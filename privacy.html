Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Ackerman Communications (“we”) handles personal information when you use this website.

What we collect

We collect the information you choose to send us through our contact form: your name, work email address, and your message. We don’t use this website to collect anything else about you, and we don’t use analytics or advertising trackers on it.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry and to keep a record of our correspondence with you. We don’t sell your information, or use it for marketing without your permission.

Legal basis (UK)

Under UK data protection law, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries or, where relevant, on steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract.

Who can see it

We only share your information with service providers who help us run this website and manage correspondence, such as our hosting provider, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only for as long as we need them for the purposes above, then delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the UK and certain US states, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to how we use it. UK residents may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. To make a request, please use our contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
