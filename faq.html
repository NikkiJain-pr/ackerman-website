FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does Ackerman Communications do?

We help organizations build authority, earn visibility, and protect their reputation through strategic communications and public relations. That includes PR strategy, media relations, thought leadership, corporate communications, reputation management, crisis communications, storytelling, digital PR, reactive communications, and launch PR.

Where do you work?

We work across the USA and UK.

Do you replace our in-house team?

No. We work as a flexible extension of your team, providing strategic counsel, communications expertise, media relationships, and campaign execution when and where you need it. If you don’t have an in-house team, you get senior-level expertise without the cost and complexity of building a large department.

How do you start?

We start with your business objectives, audience, market, and reputation goals. We then develop the right narrative, positioning, communications strategy, and media opportunities around them.

Can you guarantee media coverage?

No, and we’d be cautious of anyone who does. Earned coverage is an independent editorial decision. What we can do is make sure your story is relevant, credible, and in front of the right people.

How is your approach different?

Our approach is business-first, research-led, proactive and reactive, with a focus on relevance, credibility, and long-term reputation rather than media volume alone.

Can you help if we have an issue right now?

Yes, please get in touch and tell us what’s happening. Crisis and issues communications is one of our core capabilities.

Do you only work with large organizations?

No. What matters is that you have something important to say and want the right audience to hear it, and understand why it matters.

Have something important to say?

Let’s make sure the right audience hears it, and understands why it matters.
