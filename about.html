About us

Your reputation is working before you ever enter the room.

Ackerman Communications helps organizations build authority, earn visibility, and protect their reputation through strategic communications and public relations.

What we believe

We believe PR should do more than generate media mentions. It should strengthen credibility, shape perception, position leaders as trusted voices, and create meaningful visibility around the moments that matter.

We work across the USA and UK, helping organizations turn expertise and important business moments into opportunities for greater visibility, credibility, and influence.

How we think

What guides our work.

Business-first

Communications should serve what the business is trying to achieve, so that's where we start.

Research-led

We look at your audience, market and reputation before we recommend anything.

Proactive and reactive

We plan ahead for the moments that matter, and respond when the unexpected arrives.

Relevance over volume

We focus on relevance, credibility and long-term reputation, rather than media volume alone.
